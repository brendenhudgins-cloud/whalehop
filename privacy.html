WHALE HOP — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 2026-08-24

Short version: nothing about you is collected, because there is nothing here that could.

The app

The "Whale Hop" developer app posts devlog videos to the developer's own social media accounts. It collects no personal data from any third party. The only data it holds are the developer's own access tokens, stored locally on the developer's machine and revocable from the connected platform at any time. No analytics, no tracking, no databases, no sharing.

The site and the game

whalehop.net is a static page and the game runs entirely in your browser. Neither sets tracking cookies nor sends your data anywhere; the game's saves live in your browser's own local storage and never leave your machine.
